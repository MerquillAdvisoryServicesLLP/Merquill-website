Questions

Things people ask us first

How do we get started?

Send us a short note through the form below, WhatsApp or email. We will arrange a short conversation to understand your requirement before proposing anything.

How are fees decided?

After the first conversation we send a written proposal with scope and fees. Nothing is billed until both sides have agreed it in writing.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Client information is treated as confidential by default and used only for the agreed work. See our privacy policy.

Do you raise funds or hold client money?

No. Merquill does not raise or hold funds or offer securities. We help you prepare and, where suitable, introduce you to lenders and investors.

Can we take only one service?

Yes. Services can be taken individually or combined. See the services page.
