About Merquill

A new management consulting firm with a simple purpose: to help growing businesses get the fundamentals right.

Who we are

Merquill Advisory Services LLP is a management consulting firm established in 2026 and based in Bengaluru. We work with founders, owners and leadership teams who want dependable finance operations, a clear view of their compliance obligations, and the preparation needed to approach lenders and investors with confidence.

Many growing businesses are strong in product and sales but stretched on the back-office. Merquill exists to close that gap with practical systems, clear plans and consistent follow-through.

What we believe

  • Foundations come first. Growth is easier and cheaper on a sound base.
  • Plain language wins. If a report cannot be understood by the person who must act on it, it has not done its job.
  • Relationships outlast transactions. We would rather earn a second engagement than stretch the first.
