About Merquill

A new management consulting firm with a simple purpose: to help growing businesses get the fundamentals right.

Who we are

Merquill Advisory Services LLP is a management consulting firm established in 2026 and based in Bengaluru. We work with founders, owners and leadership teams who want dependable finance operations, a clear view of their compliance obligations, and the preparation needed to approach lenders and investors with confidence.

Many growing businesses are strong in product and sales but stretched on the back-office. Merquill exists to close that gap with practical systems, clear plans and consistent follow-through.

What we believe

  • Foundations come first. Growth is easier and cheaper on a sound base.
  • Plain language wins. If a report cannot be understood by the person who must act on it, it has not done its job.
  • Relationships outlast transactions. We would rather earn a second engagement than stretch the first.

Questions you may have

How do we get started?

Send us a short note through the contact page, WhatsApp or email. We will arrange a short conversation to understand your requirement before proposing anything.

How are fees decided?

After the first conversation we send a written proposal with scope and fees. Nothing is billed until both sides have agreed it in writing.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Client information is treated as confidential by default and used only for the agreed work. See our privacy policy.

Do you raise funds or hold client money?

No. Merquill does not raise or hold funds or offer securities. We help you prepare and, where suitable, introduce you to lenders and investors.

Can we take only one service?

Yes. Our services can be taken individually or combined. See the services page.

Where are you based, and can we meet?

We are based in Bengaluru. Meetings are by appointment, in person or by video call.
