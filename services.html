Merquill Advisory

Services

Eight focused service lines, each designed around a practical business need. Engagements can be taken individually or combined.

  • Finance function support
  • Accounting & reporting support
  • Process & cost reviews
  • Compliance & registration support
  • Project reports & funding readiness
  • Investor & lender introductions
  • Virtual CFO & GCC support
  • Business planning & consulting
01

Finance function support

A dependable finance back-office, set up and run the way your business actually works.

What this can include

  • Design of finance roles, workflows and month-end routines
  • Day-to-day finance operations support and coordination
  • Budget and cash-flow planning with simple, usable dashboards
  • MIS packs that show owners what is happening, not just what happened
  • Support with vendor, customer and payment follow-up routines
Best suited to: Founders and owners who want order in the numbers without building a large in-house team.
02

Accounting & reporting support

Well-organised books and management reports that decision-makers can rely on.

What this can include

  • Bookkeeping set-up and clean-up support on common accounting software
  • Chart-of-accounts design and ledger structuring
  • Periodic management reports, variance notes and trend views
  • Reconciliation support and tracking of open items
  • Preparation of information packs for lenders, investors and partners
Best suited to: Growing businesses whose records have outpaced their systems.
03

Process & cost reviews

A fresh, evidence-based look at how work gets done and where money quietly leaks.

What this can include

  • Mapping of key processes from order to cash and purchase to payment
  • Spend and cost-structure analysis with practical savings ideas
  • Identification of bottlenecks, duplication and control gaps
  • Simple standard operating procedures and checklists
  • A prioritised action plan with owners and timelines
Best suited to: Businesses facing margin pressure, rapid growth or unclear accountability.
04

Compliance & registration support

Calm, organised guidance through the registrations and recurring obligations a business must manage.

What this can include

  • Guidance on registrations relevant to your business, such as GST, Income Tax and TDS (including TAN), Udyam (MSME), Shops & Establishments and similar
  • Help in identifying what applies to your business and when
  • Compliance calendars, reminders and responsibility charts covering GST, TDS, Income Tax and other recurring due dates
  • Support in organising payment and deduction records so they are ready when due dates arrive
  • Assistance in preparing and organising the information and documents needed
  • Coordination with your existing advisers and service providers
Best suited to: New ventures and established businesses that want a clear view of what is due and who is doing it.
05

Project reports & funding readiness

Credible, well-structured project reports and a business that is ready for a lender's or investor's questions.

What this can include

  • Detailed project reports and business plans with financial projections
  • Loan-proposal support, including information packs and supporting schedules
  • Preparation for lender and investor discussions
  • Support with schemes and programmes that may suit your business
  • Readiness reviews that highlight gaps before a lender finds them
Best suited to: Entrepreneurs and MSMEs seeking bank finance, scheme support or investment.
06

Investor & lender introductions

Introductions to the right people, once your story and numbers are ready to be heard.

What this can include

  • Shortlisting of suitable lenders and investors for your stage and sector
  • Positioning of your story, ask and use of funds
  • Warm introductions through our network, where there is a genuine fit
  • Support in preparing for conversations and follow-ups
Best suited to: Businesses that have a clear funding need and are ready to approach capital providers. Merquill makes introductions only; it does not raise or hold funds, or offer securities.
07

Virtual CFO & GCC support

Senior finance thinking on a flexible basis, including for global capability centres setting up in India.

What this can include

  • Part-time strategic finance leadership: planning, forecasting and board-ready reporting
  • Finance operating-model design for global capability centres (GCCs) and captive units
  • Set-up planning for new India operations: structure, processes and governance
  • Coordination between Indian teams and overseas headquarters on reporting needs
  • Support for scaling finance operations as headcount grows
Best suited to: Startups and SMEs needing a finance lead without a full-time hire, and international groups establishing a presence in India.
08

Business planning & consulting

Strategy turned into a plan: priorities, numbers and a way of tracking progress.

What this can include

  • Business model and unit-economics review
  • Annual and multi-year business plans with financial models
  • Goal-setting, KPI frameworks and review rhythms
  • Market-entry and expansion planning
  • Sounding-board sessions for owners facing important decisions
Best suited to: Owners and leadership teams planning growth, a pivot or a new line of business.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe your situation and we will suggest where to begin. Many clients start with a short assessment and expand from there.
