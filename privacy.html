Privacy policy

How Merquill Advisory Services LLP handles personal information. Last updated 21 November 2026.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Merquill Advisory Services LLP (LLPIN ADB-9830), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Merquill", "we", "us"). We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it, as applicable.

2. What we collect

  • Information you send us by email, WhatsApp, phone or the enquiry form: for example your name, company, contact details and the nature of your requirement.
  • Information shared during engagements, only as needed to deliver the agreed work.

This website does not use advertising trackers, and it does not set analytics or marketing cookies. The enquiry form opens your own email application; the website itself does not store what you type.

3. How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver services you request, meet legal obligations and maintain business records. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, email and messaging platforms) and where required by law, or with your agreement. Messages sent by WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or as the law requires, and use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, and may withdraw consent for its processing. Write to us at the address below.

7. Grievance contact

Our Grievance Officer's details are given at the foot of this page.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
