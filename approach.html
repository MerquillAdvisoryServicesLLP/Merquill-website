Merquill Advisory

Our approach

How we work with you, from the first conversation to the final review.

Principles we hold ourselves to

Understand before advising

We spend time learning how your business really operates before suggesting anything. Advice that ignores context rarely survives contact with reality.

Fit the solution to the size

A ten-person business does not need a large-enterprise framework. We keep tools and processes light enough to be used every week.

Say what we think, kindly

You get an honest assessment, including when a plan is not ready, a number does not hold up or we are not the right fit.

Leave you stronger

We document what we build, explain why, and equip your team so that you are not dependent on us.

Respect confidentiality

Business and financial information is handled with care, shared only where needed and with your knowledge.

Be clear about our role

Our work is advisory and supportive in nature. We state plainly what each engagement covers and what remains with you and your other advisers.

The engagement journey

Initial conversation

No-obligation discussion of your situation, objectives and timelines. You will leave with a view on whether and how we can help.

Scoping & proposal

A written proposal: objectives, deliverables, timeline, information needed from you, and fees. Work begins only once it is agreed.

Delivery

Structured work with agreed checkpoints. You receive progress updates and have one point of contact throughout.

Review & next steps

A closing review of what was delivered, what changed and what to do next, whether on your own or with our continued support.

Engagement models

  • Project-based: a defined outcome such as a project report or a process review, for an agreed fee.
  • Retainer: ongoing support on a monthly basis for finance operations, compliance coordination or virtual CFO needs.
  • Advisory sessions: time-boxed sounding-board discussions for specific decisions.

What we ask of you

  • A single decision-maker or point of contact
  • Timely access to the information and people we need
  • Candour about constraints, concerns and priorities
  • Prompt feedback at the agreed checkpoints

Let's talk about what your business needs next

Tell us where you are and where you want to be. We will respond with an honest view of how we can help, and an equally honest view when we cannot.
